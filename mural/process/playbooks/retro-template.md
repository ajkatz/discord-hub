Every retro reads the ledger, not vibes. Standing sections:

1. **Numbers** — docs, gates run/failed, bounces, coordination:build ratio, calibration state.
2. **Stimulators observed** — what reduced CW, with ledger/commit evidence → bolster action.
3. **Detractors observed** — what inflated CW, with evidence → removal action.
4. **Graded interventions** — every action from the previous retro: did its predicted effect materialize? (Same closed loop as calibration — an ungraded intervention is a wish.)
5. **Process ADRs filed** — anything that changed the rules.
