Law 6 applies to our own prose: **a hat definition or playbook is wrong until an agent
evidenced compliance under pressure.**

- **Doc TDD:** before authoring/editing, run the baseline: a doc-less agent under combined
  pressure (deadline + sunk cost + authority). Capture its rationalizations VERBATIM.
  Write the minimal doc against those exact rationalizations. Re-test to green.
- **Micro-test wording:** no-guidance control first (if the control doesn't fail, don't
  author anything); 5+ fresh-context reps per variant; variance across reps IS the
  bindingness metric.
- **Match the form to the failure:** hard rules for skipped steps ("No exceptions" lists);
  observable-predicate conditionals for judgment ("if X is visible, then…"); checklists
  for omissions; rationalization tables for negotiated constraints.
- **Descriptions never summarize the workflow** — an index one-liner that summarizes a
  procedure BECOMES the procedure (agents act on the summary and skip the doc). One-liners
  state the trigger, never the steps.
- **Persuasion prose is the fallback layer**, used only in judgment zones exit codes
  cannot reach (debugging conduct, review reception, packet-constraint negotiation).
  Where a gate can exist, build the gate.
